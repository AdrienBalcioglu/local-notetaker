Local Notetaker

Sticky notes that clean up after themselves, and voice notes transcribed on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

No notes. Write one, or record one.

How it works

Notes live in this browser only. A note past its time is deleted the next time the page is open; choose "Keep" for the ones that matter. Voice notes are transcribed by a speech model that runs on this device: the model is downloaded once, your audio never leaves the computer, and the recording itself is not stored, only the text.
